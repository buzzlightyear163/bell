    Every trade feeds the pot.

    Wall Street closes. The board doesn't.

    Tokenized stocks keep trading onchain overnight. Whichever one has the best 24-hour move takes the lead.

  3. 09:30

    The bell rings.

    The pot buys the leader and splits it across every holder by bag size. Then it starts filling again.
