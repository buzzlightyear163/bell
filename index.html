    One pot. One bell. Every weekday.

    Times are New York time. The dial above shows where the day is right now.

    1. All day

      Trades fill the pot

      Every buy and sell of $BELL pays a fee. 80% goes into the pot. 20% buys $BELL back and burns it.

    2. 16:00 to 09:30

      The board forms overnight

      Wall Street closes, but tokenized stocks keep trading onchain. The stock with the best 24-hour move takes the lead.

    3. 09:30

      The bell rings

      The pot buys the leader on Jupiter and splits it across every holder by bag size. Then it starts filling again.

    No bell on weekends or market holidays. The pot keeps filling, so the next bell is bigger. Monday bells carry the whole weekend.
